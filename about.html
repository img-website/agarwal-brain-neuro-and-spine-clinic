The training behind the title

Eleven years of medical
education — condensed

Neurology super-specialisation in India is one of the longest and most competitive paths in all of medicine. This is the road behind the name on the door.

  1. 2011

    MBBS

    Rajiv Gandhi University of Health Sciences

    The foundational medical degree — five and a half years including a rotating internship across every major clinical department.

  2. 2015

    MD — Paediatrics

    Government Medical College, Surat

    A full postgraduate specialisation in child health. This is why paediatric seizures, febrile fits and developmental concerns are handled here with unusual depth.

  3. 2022

    DM — Neurology

    IPGMER / SSKM Hospital (PGI), Kolkata

    Three years of super-specialist neurology at one of India's busiest tertiary referral hospitals — stroke units, epilepsy monitoring, movement disorder and neuromuscular clinics.

  4. DrNB

    DrNB — Neurology

    National Board of Examinations · training incl. SGPGI Lucknow

    A second super-specialist neurology credential, independently examined at national level, with training exposure at SGPGI Lucknow.

  5. Today — Alwar

    Agarwal Brain Neuro & Spine Clinic, Arya Nagar

    Metro-standard neurology, delivered locally — so families in Alwar and across Rajasthan no longer need a day-long trip to Jaipur or Delhi for a specialist opinion.

How he practises

Four commitments to
every patient

  • Diagnosis before prescription

    Most neurological answers come from a careful history and a proper bedside examination — not from a scan. Tests are ordered to confirm a suspicion, never to go fishing.

  • Explained in your language

    Every diagnosis is explained in plain Hindi or English — what it is, why it happened, what happens next. You should leave the room understanding your own illness.

  • No unnecessary tests

    An MRI is expensive. If the result will not change your treatment, it will not be ordered. Consultation fees are kept accessible at roughly ₹300–₹500.

  • Long-term follow-up

    Epilepsy, Parkinson's and migraine are journeys, not single visits. Treatment is reviewed and re-tuned over time — you are not handed a prescription and forgotten.
