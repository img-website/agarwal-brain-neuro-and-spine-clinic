When should you see a neurologist?

If any of these describe you or someone at home, book a consultation. In neurology, early is everything.

  • 🧠

    Sudden weakness

    On one side of the body, face or arm — call immediately.

  • ⚡

    Fits or blackouts

    Any seizure, staring spell or unexplained loss of consciousness.

  • 💥

    Worst headache ever

    Or a headache pattern that has suddenly changed.

  • 🦵

    Numbness or tingling

    Persistent pins-and-needles in hands, feet or legs.

  • 🤲

    Shaking hands

    Tremor at rest, stiffness, or walking that has slowed down.

  • 🔁

    Memory slipping

    Repeating questions, losing the way, personality change.

  • 🌀

    Vertigo & imbalance

    Spinning sensation, repeated falls or unsteady walking.

  • 🔥

    Back or neck pain

    Radiating into an arm or leg — likely a nerve, not a muscle.
