What you can expect

Promises we're happy
to be judged on

Rather than quoting ourselves, here is the standard we hold — hold us to it, then review us honestly.

  • You won't be rushed

    A neurological history takes time to take properly. The examination is not shortened to clear a queue.

  • You'll understand the answer

    Explanations in plain Hindi or English — what it is, why it happened, and what the plan is from here.

  • No test you don't need

    If a scan won't change the treatment, it won't be ordered. Consultation stays around ₹300–₹500.

  • Surgery is the last resort

    A neurologist is a physician, not a surgeon. There is no incentive here to send you to an operating theatre.

  • Follow-up, not forgetting

    Chronic neurological illness needs review over months and years — treatment is re-tuned as you change.

  • Children are welcome

    With an MD in Paediatrics alongside the DM in Neurology, young patients are properly at home here.
