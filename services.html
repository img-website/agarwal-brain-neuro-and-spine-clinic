Services & treatments

Twelve specialities.
One super-specialist.

The brain, spine and nerves are one connected system — which is why they should be read by one doctor who understands all of it. Filter below to find your concern.

दिमाग, रीढ़ और नसों से जुड़ी हर समस्या का इलाज — एक ही जगह।

All treatments

12 treatments shown

  • Emergency

    Stroke & Paralysis

    Brain attack care — sudden one-sided weakness, facial droop or slurred speech. Immediate assessment, thrombolysis referral where indicated, secondary prevention and a structured recovery plan.

    • Ischaemic & haemorrhagic stroke
    • TIA / mini-stroke
    • Post-stroke rehabilitation planning

    ● Suspected stroke? Go to an emergency room now — do not wait.

  • Epilepsy & Seizures

    Not every fit is epilepsy, and not every epilepsy looks like a fit. EEG-guided classification decides the drug — the right one often controls seizures completely.

    • Adult & childhood epilepsy
    • Febrile fits, absence & staring spells
    • Drug-resistant epilepsy review

  • Migraine & Headache

    Daily painkillers make headaches worse over time — a trap called medication-overuse headache. Preventive therapy plus trigger mapping reduces how often attacks come at all.

    • Migraine with & without aura
    • Cluster & tension-type headache
    • Medication-overuse headache

  • Spine, Disc & Sciatica

    A neurologist is a physician, not a surgeon — so the plan starts with medicine, physiotherapy and posture. Most slip discs settle without an operation.

    • Slip disc & disc herniation
    • Sciatica & cervical radiculopathy
    • Neck & low back pain with tingling

  • Parkinson's & Movement

    Tremor, stiffness and slowing are highly treatable — but only when the dose is titrated to your response and reviewed as the disease changes.

    • Parkinson's disease
    • Essential tremor
    • Dystonia & gait disorders

  • Memory & Dementia

    Some memory loss is caused by thyroid disease, B12 deficiency or depression — and is fully reversible once found. That is why forgetfulness deserves a proper work-up, not a shrug.

    • Alzheimer's & vascular dementia
    • Reversible causes screening
    • Behaviour & personality change

  • Neuropathy & Weakness

    Burning feet, numb hands or muscles that are losing power. Nerve conduction studies pinpoint where the fault sits and how far it has progressed.

    • Diabetic & nutritional neuropathy
    • Guillain-Barré, myasthenia, myopathy
    • Carpal tunnel & nerve entrapment

  • Vertigo & Imbalance

    The critical question is whether spinning comes from the inner ear or the brain. That distinction changes everything — and it is answered by examination, not guesswork.

    • BPPV & vestibular neuritis
    • Vestibular migraine
    • Unsteadiness & recurrent falls
  • Rare combo

    Paediatric Neurology

    Dr. Agrawal holds an MD in Paediatrics as well as a DM in Neurology — an unusual pairing that means your child is seen by one doctor trained in both.

    • Childhood epilepsy & febrile fits
    • Developmental delay & cerebral palsy
    • School headaches & weakness

  • Sleep, Anxiety & Mood

    Poor sleep, anxiety and low mood frequently travel with neurological illness — sometimes as a cause, sometimes as a consequence. Both ends get addressed.

    • Insomnia & restless legs
    • Anxiety & depression with neuro symptoms
    • Sleep-related movement disorders

  • MS & Demyelination

    Multiple sclerosis and related conditions often begin with vague, scattered symptoms in young adults. Early recognition genuinely alters the long-term outcome.

    • Multiple sclerosis
    • Optic neuritis & NMOSD
    • Transverse myelitis
  • Emergency

    Brain Infections

    Fever with headache, neck stiffness, confusion or a first-ever fit in an adult needs assessment the same day. These are treatable — but only against the clock.

    • Meningitis & encephalitis
    • Neurocysticercosis & TB of the brain
    • Brain abscess
Investigations

Tests are guided, not guessed

Each investigation is arranged only when its result would genuinely change your treatment.

  • MRI & CT

    Structural imaging of brain and spine — arranged and, crucially, interpreted in the context of your examination.

  • EEG

    Records the brain's electrical activity to classify seizures and select the right anti-epileptic drug.

  • NCV & EMG

    Nerve conduction and muscle studies that locate exactly where a nerve is compressed or damaged.

  • Blood & CSF

    Screens for the reversible causes — B12, thyroid, sugar, infection — that mimic serious brain disease.

Not sure which one fits you?

That is exactly what the consultation is for. Describe the symptom — the diagnosis is Dr. Agrawal's job.
